My Thoughts

It legitimately took me 3 weeks to get into just 20% of this book. It finally started picking up for me after seeing just how dysfunctional Ezra's family is and how he copes to keep me from losing it, coping with Dani's indecisions/defensiveness. I made several comments throughout the first and mid-part of the book about the characters honestly seeking therapy. Dani's inability to work through and face her abandonment issues and relationship trauma was frustrating to the point I could not enjoy the book for the longest part.

I honestly read the rest of the 80% in one day (crazy enough, the ball kept rolling once it started). I think I was being harsh with Dani's defensiveness and avoiding the issues she had head-on. Ezra's issues, I can see how hard he was cornered, especially with how unsupported he is professionally and personally.

The good about the book outweighed the rough start.

I'm glad the issues were worked through slowly but surely throughout the book with a satisfying ending. I liked the interactions between the two leads and their biting banter. I, would have loved a more charming first impression of Dani, but she got better as she started to process and unpack her traumas. When things got intense between the two of them, it got INTENSE. On a side note, I loved Nate and Vera's quips and supportive roles. I'll miss them when I think back on this book in the future. The case being solved and disputed was also very much satisfying to see close and open up new opportunities. The Casaragi case was a bit bland, but I adored Mrs.Casaragi's strength and resolve to bring that Lorenzo scumbag to his knees. It was genuinely satisfying to see how things unfolded and the plot thickened.
