My Thoughts

I picked this up on Kindle Unlimited to try my hand at some patterns. I like the concept of making a square a day and making a quilt out of all the tiny projects. I did not have a sashiko needle, but using what I had, I was able to get started with the patterns and measurements listed within the book. It's a good starting point if you have the time to try something new, but you would need to know a basic level of sewing and how to use a sewing machine to keep the squares from falling apart and then sew them together when making the quilt.

I'll be working through the patterns slowly (cause a 4 by 4 inch square daily for someone who doesn't sew often is still a challenge, but the tiny squares are a good way to tackle it one at a time.)
