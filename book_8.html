My Thoughts

In honor of Valentine's Month(?) I decided to read this silly little book series Scared Sexy just for giggles so here's book one!

Clocking in at under 100 pages, this book had no business having as much depth and charm as it did. I’m a known sucker (pun fully intended) for enemies-to-lovers, and this delivered exactly what I crave. Tell me you wouldn’t fall a little bit for a man who’s had a massive crush on you for centuries—so intense that he hunts you down through time, stabs you every chance he gets just to see you, and would gladly kill anyone who gets in the way. Completely unhinged. Completely effective. I was in a chokehold.

Dirty jokes aside, I knew going in that this wouldn’t be a serious read, but it was genuinely fun. The story made it easy to imagine the centuries of pining and tangled obsession between these two long before the book ever begins. Ethyl’s backstory was surprisingly tragic and clearly explained her need to belong—to have people she could call her own. Lazlo’s backstory, particularly how his crush on Ethyl developed, was oddly adorable despite the violence baked into their dynamic. Against my better judgment, I fully condoned their murderous enemies-to-lovers arc.

The ending/climax reminded me of [b:Bride|181344829|Bride (Bride, #1)|Ali Hazelwood|https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1730451646l/181344829._SY75_.jpg|185069024] by Ali Hazelwood, and honestly? It worked. It felt natural (even if it's a paranormal story), satisfying, and perfectly tied up loose ends neatly. For something short, silly, and unapologetically chaotic, Hot for Slayer was an absolute delight.
