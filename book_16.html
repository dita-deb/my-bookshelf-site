My Thoughts

I read Bride (Twice) and loved it both times. It's cliché with its many common tropes, sweet characters, and the spicy but tender romance. I ADORED IT.

This book... was very different in its approach. I, off the bat, completely hated both Serena and Koen. Koen because he was a special fucker.. an exceptional asshole, but he was truthful (ish) and dependable at the very least. Serena was a straight-up LIAR for the majority of the book. I could not understand for the life of me how Misery could jump into a marriage with the original intention of finding and reuniting with her adoptive sister and bestie Serena, but Serena, slowly crumpling with little to no time, refused to even share the rest of her time with Misery at the very least?? The selfishness was absolutely insane. I got why she felt as she did, but gods... I was finding it unbearable for the longest.

I was genuinely praying for someone to fold first cause the secondhand embarrassment I was experiencing through Serena was unfounded. Koen folded around the 50% mark, making the rest of the novel much more entertaining and a breath of hope for the story and characters. Koen and Serena's relationship was and is very rough and intense. It was vastly different from Lowe and Misery's relationship, which was much more titillating and romantic.

(I'm down bad for MiseryxLowe, so I loved every one of Misery's inclusion and involvement throughout the book. It was very grounding to see Misery and Serena's relationship bonding too.)

Truthfully, I gave Bride 4 stars originally, but even after the second read, it was entertaining and thoroughly scratched that nostalgic 2010s Wattpad-era itch. Therefore, I'd revised my rating to 5 stars. Now, regarding this book, it concluded well, and the story and lore were phenomenal. I loved how it was time-lined and switching back and forth till it caught up to the present. I loved the ongoing jokes, banter, and teasing. I just was not expecting so much more dark, intense lusting and roughness, it was almost borderline toxicity rather than mutual love.

Though it worked out, and honestly, it was a good ending. Since it did make me happy, especially the little nuances and moments that carried over from the first book, I did like the book overall. 4/5 stars for now, but that is subject to change as I see fit.
