My Thoughts

That ending was criminal.

It honestly took until the pub conversations for me to feel genuinely invested in their (Brigan, our fallen angel, and Catalina) relationship. Up until then, the physical attraction was obvious, and the chemistry was there, but it wasn’t enough to fully pull me in. It was those quieter moments—being honest over drinks, trading witty banter, actually talking—that made them feel real. That’s when I started rooting for them.

Which is exactly why the abrupt ending was so frustrating.

The story cuts off just shy of any meaningful resolution. For something that hinges on a curse, there’s surprisingly little clarity about how it could be broken—or if it even was. Are we supposed to assume Brigan’s curse was somehow undone? Was she meant to become immortal to stay with him? None of it is clearly addressed, which makes the conclusion feel incomplete rather than open-ended.

I wouldn’t say I hated the book, but I disliked it. The stakes were relatively low (considering this is just a silly Romance series set in Halloween that I'm reading in the wrong season, but I digress), and there were genuinely enjoyable moments. But coming off [b:Hot for Slayer|240581511|Hot for Slayer|Ali Hazelwood|https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1756061884l/240581511._SY75_.jpg|258642212], which had surprising depth, satisfying character arcs, and a conclusive ending, my expectations were clearly set high for the series overall. In comparison, this one felt underdeveloped—particularly in its final act.

The potential was there. The emotional groundwork was there. The execution at the end just didn’t quite follow through for me.

For now, I’ll mentally fantasize and give the characters the ending they deserve.
