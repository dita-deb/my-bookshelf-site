My Thoughts

It's been a while since I've finished reading this book and honestly it wasn't bad. I just didn't think too hard about it as I read and actually missed that he had a tail the entire time which was hilarious after I read some reviews on the book. I believe that this particular book in the series was more smutty compared to the others before it.

I'll give the book credit in that it was very well structured and had great chemistry between the two characters actually getting along for their benefit then slowly considering the other's comfort over their own as they got closer to one another. It was a great close to the story as well so I'm not mad at the story in the least so it will get a 3/5 from me.
