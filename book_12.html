My Thoughts

I was honestly not feeling it for a minute at the start because I had the idea of monsters inc seered into my brain when I picked it up. I do have to say I was not expecting that reveal at the end to be what it was... It was a very short read, and it was interesting how it played out. I'm not saying it was great, but it was a good read for a short story that reached the end and left no loopholes. So I'll give it a 3/5. I do wish there were different perspectives to further add to the depth of the characters but it was fine as it was.
